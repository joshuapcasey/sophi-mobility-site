Technology Platform

A Fully Integrated
Valet Tech Ecosystem

Technology isn't just a tool at SOPHI, it's our foundation. Our systems don't just collect data, they talk to each other for unmatched efficiency, speed, and insight.

SMS Valet Requests

Paperless ticketing. Guests text to request their vehicle and receive real-time notifications with estimated delivery time. Works from anywhere, including while landing at the airport.

Real-Time KPI Dashboards

Live tracking of vehicles parked, retrieval times, capture rates, revenue by card type, labor costs, and employee leaderboards. Data flows from operations to payroll to reporting.

LPR Enforcement

Mobile license plate recognition with AI/ML-powered make, model, and color detection. Average citation issuance in 47 seconds. Virtual appeals portal included.

Workforce Management

Cloud-based scheduling, shift swapping, attendance tracking, and employee performance scoring across 5 KPIs. Feeds directly into payroll via Gusto integration.

Text-to-Park (App-Free)

No app downloads needed. Guests text a zone code, enter their plate, and pay via phone. Extend sessions remotely. Built-in marketing platform with validation programs for local businesses.

Damage Documentation

Cloud-based photo system captures all vehicle conditions at arrival. Damage tied to license plates protects clients from fraudulent claims. Full 7-step incident protocol.
